# Task 04 — FinOps Dashboard

## What I Built
A cost governance setup: a monthly budget with three escalating
alert thresholds, and a KQL query for cost attribution by resource group.

## Budget Alert Thresholds
- 70% — Warning. Review spend trend, no action required yet.
- 90% — Action required. Freeze non-essential resource creation
  and investigate the cause of the spend increase.
- 100% — Escalate immediately to the budget owner and finance team.

## Why This Matters
Cost visibility without action thresholds is just a dashboard nobody
looks at. Defining what happens at each threshold turns a budget alert
into an actual operational process with clear escalation paths.
